Policy hierarchy, effective dates, amendment, supersession, applicability, exceptions, activation and conflict escalation are modeled. Retroactive/transition provisions and temporary issuances are not sufficiently specified for schema or execution. The registry may store verified metadata; it must not invent precedence or legal effect.
