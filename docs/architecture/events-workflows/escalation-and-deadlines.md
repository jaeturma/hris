# Escalation and Deadlines

## Purpose

Define deadline types, reminder intervals, grace periods, escalation levels, delegated authority, and audit requirements.

## Deadline Types

| Type | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Policy deadline | Deadline required by policy or issuance. | Publication period, submission window, CSC-related deadline. |
| Office service-level target | Internal target for operational responsiveness. | HR screening within local service target. |
| Internal reminder target | Reminder-only date before a deadline. | Notify evaluator before due date. |
| Legal or regulatory deadline | Deadline with legal/regulatory consequence. | Records or CSC submission timeframe where verified. |

Do not treat all deadlines as interchangeable.

## Required Concepts

Due dates, reminder intervals, grace periods, escalation levels, acting or delegated authority, approver unavailability, office holidays, non-working days, suspended deadlines, manual overrides, override justification, and audit logs.

## Escalation Levels

| Level | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 0 | Reminder | Notify assigned actor. |
| Level 1 | Deadline warning | Notify actor and supervisor/secretariat. |
| Level 2 | Overdue escalation | Notify office head or workflow owner. |
| Level 3 | Authority escalation | Reassign or route to delegated/acting authority if policy permits. |
| Level 4 | Compliance flag | Create governance/compliance follow-up. |

## Suspension Rules

Deadlines may be suspended only by policy, official office closure, returned-for-compliance status, authorized hold, or workflow configuration. Suspension and resumption must be logged.

## Override Rules

Manual overrides require permission, reason, actor, office context, original deadline, new deadline, policy reference, and audit record.
