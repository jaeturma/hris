# Career Progression Records

## Purpose

Define records for higher teaching positions, expanded career progression, Master Teacher and School Principal pathways, reclassification, retitling, conversion, and transition handling.

## Record Types

Application, supporting evidence, qualification evidence, competency evidence, performance evidence, reclassification evaluation, career-stage evaluation, assessment results, ranking, recommendation, approval, retitling, transition record, and historical decision snapshot.

## Rules

- Each completed action must reference DepEd Order, Memorandum, CSC, policy version, assessment framework, QS version, competency framework, and effectivity period.
- Career progression documents must not collapse all pathways into generic promotion records.
- Historical performance-rating and competency evidence snapshots must be preserved.
- Retitling, position conversion, reclassification, and appointment outputs require distinct document categories.

## Access

Career progression documents are high or restricted sensitivity because they often include performance, competency, HRMPSB, and salary/position evidence.
