# Performance Management

Performance management must connect SPMS-aligned ratings with competency expectations.

## Linkages

- Performance ratings should reference the relevant period and role.
- Competency expectations should come from the position profile.
- Behavioral indicators should guide evidence collection.
- Gaps should feed Individual Development Plans.
- Strong performance and demonstrated competencies may support promotion, succession, rewards, or recognition.

## Controls

Performance and competency records are sensitive. Access must be permission-gated, office-scoped, and audit-logged.
