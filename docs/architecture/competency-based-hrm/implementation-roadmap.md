# CBHRMS Implementation Roadmap

## Stage 1: Governance and Definitions

Define competency governance, roles, terminology, and source policy references.

## Stage 2: Competency Library

Create competency model, dictionary, categories, proficiency levels, and behavioral indicators.

## Stage 3: Position Profiles

Map job families, positions, plantilla items, and competency requirements.

## Stage 4: HR Lifecycle Integration

Integrate competencies into recruitment, selection, placement, performance, learning and development, promotion, reclassification, succession, and rewards.

## Stage 5: Reporting and PRIME-HRM Readiness

Produce competency gap analytics, HR maturity evidence, SPMS alignment views, PRAISE alignment views, and office-scoped readiness reports.

## Stage 6: AI Assistance

Introduce AI-assisted analysis only with human review, transparent evidence, permission controls, and audit trail.
