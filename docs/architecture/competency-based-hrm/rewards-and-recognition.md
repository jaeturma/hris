# Rewards and Recognition

Rewards and recognition should align with performance, demonstrated competencies, and PRAISE principles.

## Linkages

- Performance Rating Linkage
- Rewards and Recognition Linkage
- Demonstrated competency evidence
- Behavioral indicators
- Office scope
- Approval workflow

## Rules

- Do not grant rewards solely from automated scores.
- Preserve evidence and approval trail.
- Make recognition reports permission-aware.
- Link records to performance and competency evidence where applicable.
