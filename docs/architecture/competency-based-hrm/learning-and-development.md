# Learning and Development

Learning and development must be driven by competency gaps and role expectations.

## Individual Development Plan

An IDP should reference:

- Employee
- Position profile
- Competency gaps
- Target proficiency
- Learning and development interventions
- Timeline
- Responsible office or supervisor
- Completion evidence

## L&D Intervention

Interventions may include training, coaching, mentoring, job exposure, self-directed learning, or other development activities. The system should track the competency gap addressed and completion evidence.

## Reporting

Reports should show office-scoped gap patterns, intervention completion, and development progress without exposing unnecessary sensitive details.
