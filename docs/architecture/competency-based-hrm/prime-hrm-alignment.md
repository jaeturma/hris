# PRIME-HRM Alignment

The HRIS must support meritocracy and excellence in government HRM by producing structured, auditable evidence for HR maturity and compliance.

## Alignment Areas

- Recruitment, selection, and placement must use qualification standards, position profiles, competency requirements, and HRMPSB evidence.
- Performance management must link ratings, expectations, behavioral indicators, and competency evidence.
- Learning and development must respond to competency gaps through Individual Development Plans.
- Rewards and recognition must link to demonstrated performance and competencies.

## Engineering Rules

- Do not hardcode policy scoring or ranking rules without current official confirmation.
- Keep competency evidence traceable to position profiles and assessment periods.
- Preserve audit history for HR decisions.
- Make reports office-scoped and permission-controlled.
- Support PRIME-HRM readiness reporting without exposing sensitive employee details unnecessarily.
