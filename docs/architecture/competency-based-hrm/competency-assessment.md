# Competency Assessment

Competency assessment records evidence of proficiency against position expectations.

## Assessment Inputs

- Position profile
- Required competencies
- Proficiency levels
- Behavioral indicators
- Evidence sources
- Assessor and assessment period
- Employee or applicant context

## Outputs

- Assessed proficiency
- Competency gaps
- Strengths
- Development needs
- Promotion readiness indicators
- Succession readiness indicators

## Controls

Assessments are sensitive HR records. They require role-based access, office scope, audit trail, and careful export controls.
