# 25 AI Automation Opportunities

## Purpose

Define a governance process for identifying, prioritizing, testing, and approving automation and AI assistance opportunities across the HRIS.

## Trigger

Repeated manual workload, backlog, audit finding, management request, user feedback, process bottleneck, or sufficient structured data for safe assistance.

## Actors

HR leadership, process owner, data protection role, system administrator, AI reviewer, records officer, auditor, end-user representative.

## Inputs

Business process documents, workflow logs, data quality reports, user pain points, risk assessment, policy configuration, security requirements, and sample outputs.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Identify process pain point or repeatable decision support need.
2. Classify as automation, AI assistance, or both.
3. Assess data readiness, privacy risk, policy risk, and human review requirement.
4. Pilot low-risk assistance with controlled users and audit logging.
5. Validate output quality, bias, traceability, and user value.
6. Approve, revise, defer, or reject the opportunity.

## Decision Points

- Is the task deterministic automation or judgment-assisting AI?
- Is data structured, current, and authorized for use?
- Could output affect employment, ranking, promotion, discipline, or benefits?
- What human review and audit trail is required?

## Outputs

Automation opportunity backlog, AI use case register, risk assessment, pilot results, approval decision, monitoring report.

## Applicable Policy Area

Data Privacy Act, government records management, CSC merit principles, DepEd HR policies, PRIME-HRM, CBHRMS, internal ICT and AI governance.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports maturity, efficiency, evidence quality, and strategic HR management across all PRIME-HRM areas.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Prioritizes AI assistance for competency gap summaries, IDP drafting, learning recommendations, promotion readiness summaries, and succession planning support.

## System Responsibilities

Keep AI optional, reviewable, explainable, permission-aware, logged, and never the sole decision-maker for employment actions.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

Very High - many HRIS processes have repetitive routing, validation, reminders, status checks, and reports.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - some areas are ready for summaries and checks, while employment-impacting recommendations require stronger governance.

## AI Opportunities

Applicant packet summaries, missing document detection, competency gap summaries, IDP suggestions, audit packet preparation, retirement forecasts, policy-aware help, and report narratives.

## Reports Produced

Automation backlog, AI use case register, risk assessment report, pilot evaluation, monitoring dashboard.

## Security Considerations

Do not expose restricted data to unauthorized AI contexts; log prompts and outputs where appropriate; require human review for sensitive decisions.

## Future Integrations

AI Assistant, Document Management, Email, SMS, Learning Management, Mobile Application, Government Portals.

## Related Business Processes

All business processes, especially high-volume recruitment, document management, leave, attendance, competency, and PRIME-HRM evidence processes.
