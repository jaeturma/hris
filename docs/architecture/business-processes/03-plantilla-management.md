# 03 Plantilla Management

## Purpose

Maintain official plantilla items, item numbers, position titles, salary grades, office assignments, vacancy status, and relationship to competency-based position profiles.

## Trigger

New item, abolished item, vacant item, appointment, transfer, promotion, reclassification, correction, or annual plantilla validation.

## Actors

Plantilla manager, HR officer, budget personnel, office head, records officer, approving authority, auditor.

## Inputs

Plantilla item records, item numbers, salary grades, position titles, employment statuses, appointment records, office or station data, and competency profile mappings.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Register or verify plantilla item details.
2. Link item to office, station, position title, salary grade, and job family.
3. Assign vacancy, encumbered, abolished, or reserved status.
4. Link item to position competency profile and qualification standard.
5. Update item through appointment, movement, promotion, reclassification, or separation events.
6. Preserve history and produce official plantilla reports.

## Decision Points

- Is the item authorized and active?
- Is the item vacant, encumbered, or subject to action?
- Does the item match the position title, salary grade, QS, and competency profile?
- Which office has authority to update or certify the item?

## Outputs

Plantilla masterlist, vacancy list, item history, encumbrance record, and position profile linkage.

## Applicable Policy Area

DepEd staffing and appointment guidance; CSC Qualification Standards; government records management; PRIME-HRM; CBHRMS.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports Recruitment, Selection, and Placement by maintaining position integrity and evidence for appointments.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Each item should connect to a position profile with required core, leadership, and functional / technical competencies.

## System Responsibilities

Maintain immutable item identifiers, effective-dated history, vacancy status, office scope, audit trail, and dependency checks before recruitment or movement.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - status updates and vacancy derivation can be automated from approved appointment and movement records.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can flag inconsistencies, but official plantilla corrections require human validation.

## AI Opportunities

Detect duplicate item numbers, inconsistent salary grade mapping, missing competency profiles, and mismatches between appointment and item records.

## Reports Produced

Plantilla masterlist, vacant item list, encumbered item list, item history, and position competency coverage report.

## Security Considerations

Limit editing to authorized plantilla roles; log all corrections and status changes; protect employee-linked item data.

## Future Integrations

Payroll, Government Portals, Document Management, AI Assistant.

## Related Business Processes

Workforce Planning, Recruitment Selection Appointment, Reclassification, Personnel Movement, Service Record Management.
