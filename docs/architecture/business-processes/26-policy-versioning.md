# 26 Policy Versioning

## Purpose

Define the policy registry and versioning process for DepEd Orders, CSC issuances, Regional Memoranda, Division Memoranda, local implementing guidance, and internal HR workflow configurations.

## Trigger

New issuance, amendment, clarification, supersession, local memorandum, transitory provision, policy effectivity date, or workflow configuration change.

## Actors

HR policy owner, Regional or Division HR, legal or authorized policy reviewer, system administrator, records officer, auditor.

## Inputs

Policy title, issuing authority, issuance number, effectivity date, supersession details, local implementing memorandum, affected workflow, transition notes, and approved configuration references.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Register national policy, CSC issuance, or local memorandum.
2. Identify affected workflows, position groups, offices, documents, criteria, outputs, and reports.
3. Record effectivity period, superseded issuances, amendments, and transitory provisions.
4. Link policy version to assessment frameworks, QS versions, competency frameworks, MSP versions, and report templates.
5. Approve configuration for future cycles.
6. Preserve prior policy versions for historical evaluations.

## Decision Points

- Is the issuance national, regional, division, school/IU-level, or internal?
- Does it amend, clarify, supersede, or locally implement another policy?
- Which cycles are governed by old, new, or transitory rules?
- Which configuration records must be versioned before use?

## Outputs

Policy registry, policy version record, supersession map, effectivity map, affected workflow list, validation flags.

## Applicable Policy Area

DepEd Orders and Memoranda, CSC Memorandum Circulars including appointment and HR action rules, PRIME-HRM, CBHRMS, Data Privacy Act, government records management.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports process maturity, traceability, compliance evidence, and merit-based HR governance.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Connects competency frameworks, position profiles, assessment evidence, IDPs, promotion readiness, and succession readiness to the policy version active at the time.

## System Responsibilities

Maintain policy registry records, effectivity periods, supersession links, local memorandum links, version locks, transition flags, and audit logs.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - reminders, effective-date warnings, affected-workflow lists, and policy-to-framework linkage checks can be automated.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI may summarize registered policy metadata and affected workflows, but policy interpretation requires HR/legal validation.

## AI Opportunities

Policy impact summaries, configuration checklist generation, transition-risk summaries, and detection of workflows using expired versions.

## Reports Produced

Policy registry, supersession report, effectivity calendar, affected workflow report, policy validation gap report.

## Security Considerations

Restrict approval of policy configuration; log changes; preserve prior versions; require manual validation for interpretation-sensitive fields.

## Future Integrations

Document Management, Email, Government Portals, AI Assistant.

## Related Business Processes

Merit Selection Plan, HRMPSB Governance, Assessment Framework Engine, Appointment and CSC Action, PRIME-HRM Alignment.
