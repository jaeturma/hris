# 07 Non-Teaching Hiring

## Purpose

Support recruitment, selection, and appointment for non-teaching positions using CSC Qualification Standards, DepEd office needs, position profiles, and HRMPSB processes.

## Trigger

Vacant administrative, technical, professional, or support position; workforce plan; replacement need; or newly authorized item.

## Actors

Applicant, HR officer, office head, evaluator, HRMPSB, appointing authority, records officer.

## Inputs

Vacancy, position title, plantilla item, QS, competency profile, applicant profile, documents, evaluation criteria, interview results, and recommendation records.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Validate non-teaching vacancy and position profile.
2. Publish vacancy and accept applications.
3. Check documentary completeness and QS compliance.
4. Conduct assessment, interview, and competency evidence review.
5. Route to HRMPSB and appointing authority.
6. Track appointment, onboarding, assignment, and records update.

## Decision Points

- Does the applicant meet QS and required competencies?
- Which office or station owns the item?
- Which assessment criteria apply to the job family?
- Is the selected candidate cleared for appointment processing?

## Outputs

Applicant list, QS results, evaluation records, interview results, ranking or recommendation, appointment tracker, onboarding trigger.

## Applicable Policy Area

DepEd recruitment and appointment guidance; CSC Qualification Standards; PRIME-HRM; CBHRMS; Data Privacy Act; records management.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports Recruitment, Selection, and Placement through merit-based selection evidence.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Uses functional / technical competencies, core competencies, job family expectations, and competency gaps for placement and onboarding.

## System Responsibilities

Support configurable non-teaching criteria, position-to-competency profiles, HRMPSB routing, document checklists, and appointment tracking.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - QS checks, checklists, routing, and report generation are repeatable.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can summarize evidence, but specialized technical suitability needs human review.

## AI Opportunities

Applicant packet summaries, competency evidence highlighting, missing-document detection, and draft interview packet preparation.

## Reports Produced

Non-teaching applicant list, qualified applicant list, assessment summary, HRMPSB recommendation report, appointment tracker.

## Security Considerations

Apply office scope, protect applicant data, limit evaluator records, and log document access.

## Future Integrations

Document Management, Email, SMS, Digital Signature, Government Portals, AI Assistant.

## Related Business Processes

Recruitment Selection Appointment, Plantilla Management, Employee Onboarding, Competency Management.
