# 01 Human Resource Lifecycle

## Purpose

Define the complete HR lifecycle supported by the HRIS: Vacancy -> Publication -> Application -> Evaluation -> HRMPSB -> Appointment -> Onboarding -> Assignment -> Performance -> Learning -> Promotion -> Personnel Movement -> Leave -> Service Record -> Recognition -> Retirement -> Records Retention.

## Trigger

A plantilla need, vacancy, staffing plan, employee movement, career event, leave event, recognition event, separation, or records requirement.

## Actors

Applicant, employee, HR officer, HRMPSB member, evaluator, school head, office head, appointing authority, records officer, auditor, system administrator.

## Inputs

Plantilla items, position profiles, competency requirements, qualification standards, applicant or employee records, 201 file documents, performance ratings, L&D records, approvals, and policy configuration.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Identify workforce need or employee lifecycle event.
2. Confirm position, office, budget, policy, and competency requirements.
3. Collect application, employee, document, performance, or movement records.
4. Route for screening, assessment, HRMPSB action, approval, or records validation.
5. Generate official outputs, update master records, and retain evidence.
6. Feed results into service records, competency gaps, learning plans, reports, and PRIME-HRM evidence.

## Decision Points

- Is there an authorized plantilla item or approved personnel action?
- Does the person meet qualification, documentary, performance, and competency requirements?
- Which office scope and approval authority applies?
- Which records must be retained, restricted, corrected, or archived?

## Outputs

Vacancy records, publications, applications, evaluations, rankings, appointment recommendations, employee profiles, assignments, service records, leave ledgers, recognition records, separation records, and retention logs.

## Applicable Policy Area

DepEd hiring, placement, promotion, reclassification, and personnel action issuances; CSC rules and Qualification Standards; PRIME-HRM; CSC CBHRMS direction; Data Privacy Act; government records management.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Contributes to Recruitment, Selection, and Placement; Learning and Development; Performance Management; Rewards and Recognition; and HR systems maturity evidence.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Every major lifecycle event can reference required competencies, current competencies, gap analysis, individual development plans, promotion readiness, and succession readiness.

## System Responsibilities

Maintain canonical records, office scopes, workflow statuses, document history, approvals, permissions, audit logs, and report-ready data without hardcoding policy values.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

Very High - the lifecycle contains repeatable state changes, reminders, routing, document checks, and report outputs.

## AI Readiness Rating

High - AI can assist with summaries, completeness checks, gap explanations, and exception detection once records are structured and human review is required.

## AI Opportunities

Lifecycle assistant, missing-record detection, competency gap summaries, service-record draft checks, promotion readiness summaries, and audit packet preparation.

## Reports Produced

Vacancy pipeline, applicant pipeline, appointment tracker, employee masterlist, movement summary, leave summary, service record reports, PRIME-HRM evidence index, and records retention reports.

## Security Considerations

Apply office scope, least privilege, document classification, self-service boundaries, HRMPSB assignment limits, document access logs, and activity trails.

## Future Integrations

Attendance Devices, Payroll, Document Management, Email, SMS, Digital Signature, Government Portals, Learning Management, Mobile Application, AI Assistant.
