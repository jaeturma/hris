# 20 Discipline Grievance

## Purpose

Document discipline and grievance records with strict confidentiality, routing, evidence management, and audit controls without making legal conclusions.

## Trigger

Complaint, grievance filing, incident report, administrative action, request for conference, resolution record, or records update.

## Actors

Employee, complainant, respondent, supervisor, HR officer, authorized committee or official, legal or administrative officer where applicable, records officer, auditor.

## Inputs

Complaint or grievance record, incident details, involved parties, supporting documents, notices, meeting records, actions taken, resolution, and confidentiality classification.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Receive complaint, grievance, or incident record.
2. Classify sensitivity, involved parties, and authorized handlers.
3. Record required documents, notices, meetings, actions, and status.
4. Route only to authorized officials or committees.
5. Record resolution or next action as documented by authorized personnel.
6. Retain records according to classification and records rules.

## Decision Points

- Is the matter discipline, grievance, incident documentation, or another HR matter?
- Who is authorized to view or act on the record?
- What notices, meetings, documents, or actions are required?
- What records must be retained or restricted?

## Outputs

Case record, document index, action history, resolution record, access log, restricted audit trail.

## Applicable Policy Area

CSC rules, DepEd administrative and grievance procedures, Data Privacy Act, records management. Policy text must be manually verified before implementation.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports HR systems integrity, employee relations evidence, and fair process documentation.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

May inform development or behavioral interventions only when authorized and appropriate; must not be misused for broad competency scoring.

## System Responsibilities

Provide restricted case records, document history, role-based access, approval routing, status tracking, and immutable audit logs.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

Low - confidentiality and due process risks require careful manual handling, though reminders and routing can help.

## AI Readiness Rating

Low - AI use is high risk and should be limited to admin summaries only after governance approval.

## AI Opportunities

Restricted summarization of case timelines and missing document checklists, only for authorized handlers and with clear human review.

## Reports Produced

Restricted case list, status report, action timeline, document completeness report, access log report.

## Security Considerations

Highest confidentiality; isolate permissions, log access, restrict exports, protect involved parties, and avoid broad dashboard exposure.

## Future Integrations

Document Management, Email, Digital Signature, AI Assistant with strict governance.

## Related Business Processes

Performance Management, Personnel Records, Records Retention, Document Approval Routing.
