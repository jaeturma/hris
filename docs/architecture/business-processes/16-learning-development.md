# 16 Learning Development

## Purpose

Plan, track, and evaluate learning and development interventions based on competency gaps, performance needs, career progression, and office priorities.

## Trigger

Competency gap, performance development need, mandatory training, career progression plan, succession plan, office capability need, or employee IDP.

## Actors

Employee, supervisor, HR L&D officer, training provider, office head, approving authority, competency administrator.

## Inputs

Competency assessments, gap analyses, IDPs, performance ratings, training needs analysis, learning interventions, attendance, completion evidence, and evaluation results.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Identify learning needs from competency gaps, performance, or policy requirements.
2. Create or update IDP and L&D plan.
3. Approve intervention, schedule, provider, participants, and budget where applicable.
4. Track attendance, completion, evaluation, and evidence.
5. Reassess competencies or development outcomes.
6. Link outcomes to promotion readiness, succession readiness, and PRIME-HRM evidence.

## Decision Points

- Is the intervention required, recommended, or optional?
- Which competency gap or performance need does it address?
- Is the employee eligible and approved to attend?
- Did the intervention improve competency evidence?

## Outputs

IDP, L&D plan, training nomination, completion record, evaluation result, competency update, L&D reports.

## Applicable Policy Area

CSC PRIME-HRM L&D; CBHRMS; DepEd professional development guidance; Data Privacy Act; records management.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Directly supports Learning and Development and contributes to Performance Management and succession readiness.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

L&D should address required-current competency gaps and feed promotion readiness and succession readiness.

## System Responsibilities

Maintain IDPs, intervention catalog, approvals, attendance, completion evidence, evaluation, and links to competency gaps.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - IDP tracking, training nominations, completion status, reminders, and reports can be automated.

## AI Readiness Rating

High - AI can suggest learning options and summarize gaps when recommendations are reviewed by HR or supervisors.

## AI Opportunities

IDP drafting, learning intervention matching, competency gap summaries, post-training reflection prompts, and L&D analytics summaries.

## Reports Produced

Training needs analysis, IDP status report, L&D completion report, competency gap closure report, participant report.

## Security Considerations

Protect performance-linked development notes and restrict supervisor and HR access by office scope.

## Future Integrations

Learning Management, Email, SMS, Document Management, Mobile Application, AI Assistant.

## Related Business Processes

Competency Management, Performance Management, Career Progression, Rewards Recognition.
