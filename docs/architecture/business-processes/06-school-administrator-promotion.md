# 06 School Administrator Promotion

## Purpose

Manage promotion or selection for school administrator and school head roles using position requirements, performance evidence, leadership competencies, HRMPSB review, and appointment tracking.

## Trigger

Vacancy in school administrator or school head position, career progression opportunity, succession need, or approved promotion cycle.

## Actors

Employee-applicant, HR officer, school or office head, evaluator, HRMPSB, appointing authority, records officer.

## Inputs

Position profile, leadership competency requirements, qualification standard, service record, performance ratings, training, experience, documents, assessments, and promotion application.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Validate available position and promotion track.
2. Accept employee applications and supporting documents.
3. Check QS, performance, experience, training, and leadership competency evidence.
4. Conduct comparative assessment and HRMPSB evaluation.
5. Rank or recommend according to verified policy configuration.
6. Track appointment, movement, service record update, and 201 file update.

## Decision Points

- Does the employee meet QS, performance, and competency expectations?
- Which promotion or career progression policy applies?
- Is the leadership competency evidence sufficient for the position profile?
- What appointment or movement record must follow?

## Outputs

Promotion application, evaluation result, HRMPSB record, ranking or recommendation, appointment tracker, movement record, service record update.

## Applicable Policy Area

DepEd higher teaching, school administrator, promotion, career progression, appointment, and reclassification guidance; CSC rules; PRIME-HRM; CBHRMS.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports Recruitment, Selection, and Placement, Performance Management, L&D, and succession readiness.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Emphasizes leadership competencies, current proficiency, gaps, development history, promotion readiness, and succession readiness.

## System Responsibilities

Maintain promotion workflows, leadership competency profiles, evidence checklists, HRMPSB records, recommendation history, and appointment updates.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - evidence tracking, profile comparison, routing, and reports are automatable after policy details are configured.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can summarize evidence and gaps, but leadership assessment requires careful human judgment.

## AI Opportunities

Leadership evidence summaries, readiness narratives, missing evidence alerts, and competency-to-position comparison briefs.

## Reports Produced

Promotion applicant list, competency readiness report, HRMPSB packet, ranking or recommendation report, appointment tracker.

## Security Considerations

Restrict performance ratings, promotion evidence, HRMPSB deliberations, and appointment recommendations to authorized users.

## Future Integrations

Learning Management, Document Management, Digital Signature, Email, AI Assistant.

## Related Business Processes

Career Progression, Competency Management, Performance Management, Learning Development, Personnel Movement.
