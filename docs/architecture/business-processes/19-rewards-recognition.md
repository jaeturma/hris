# 19 Rewards Recognition

## Purpose

Support PRAISE-aligned rewards and recognition processes using performance, competency, service, and contribution evidence.

## Trigger

Nomination, recognition cycle, outstanding performance, service milestone, office initiative, or policy-directed awards period.

## Actors

Employee, nominator, supervisor, HR officer, PRAISE or awards committee, approving authority, records officer.

## Inputs

Nomination record, performance ratings, competency evidence, service record, supporting documents, evaluation criteria, committee action, and approval record.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Open recognition cycle or accept nomination.
2. Validate nominee eligibility and evidence.
3. Review performance, competency, service, and contribution records.
4. Route to committee or approving authority.
5. Record award decision and recognition details.
6. Update employee record and PRIME-HRM evidence.

## Decision Points

- Is the nominee eligible under the recognition category?
- Is evidence sufficient and verified?
- Does the recognition require committee review?
- Should recognition link to performance or competency record?

## Outputs

Nomination record, evaluation result, award decision, recognition record, certificate metadata, rewards report.

## Applicable Policy Area

PRAISE and related government recognition guidance; PRIME-HRM Rewards and Recognition; DepEd local recognition policies; Data Privacy Act.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Directly supports Rewards and Recognition and reinforces meritocracy and excellence.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Recognition should link to demonstrated competencies, performance results, and values-based behavior where applicable.

## System Responsibilities

Maintain nomination cycles, criteria, evidence, committee actions, approvals, recognition history, and reportable PRIME-HRM evidence.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

Medium - nomination routing and evidence collection are automatable, but award judgments require committee review.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can summarize nominee evidence but should not rank or select awardees without human committee control.

## AI Opportunities

Nomination evidence summaries, eligibility checklist checks, recognition citation draft support, and duplicate award detection.

## Reports Produced

Recognition nominee list, awardee list, committee action report, recognition history, PRIME-HRM rewards evidence report.

## Security Considerations

Protect nominations, committee notes, performance evidence, and unpublished award decisions.

## Future Integrations

Document Management, Email, Digital Signature, AI Assistant, Mobile Application.

## Related Business Processes

Performance Management, Competency Management, Learning Development, 201 Files Management.
