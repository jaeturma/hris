# 22 Records Retention

## Purpose

Manage retention, archival, disposition, access, and history of HR records according to government records management requirements.

## Trigger

Record creation, document verification, retention period review, employee separation, audit request, archival schedule, or disposition approval.

## Actors

Records officer, HR officer, data protection role, approving authority, auditor, system administrator.

## Inputs

Document types, retention rules, employee records, applicant records, workflow records, document access logs, disposition requests, and approval records.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Classify records by type, sensitivity, owner, and retention requirement.
2. Link records to employee, applicant, office, workflow, or case.
3. Track retention start, review, archival, and disposition dates.
4. Route retention or disposition actions for approval.
5. Preserve audit trail and access history.
6. Produce retention and compliance reports.

## Decision Points

- What retention rule applies to the record?
- Is the record permanent, temporary, restricted, or disposal-eligible?
- Who can approve archival or disposition?
- Does a legal, audit, or administrative hold apply?

## Outputs

Retention schedule, archival queue, disposition request, approved retention action, access history, compliance report.

## Applicable Policy Area

Government records management requirements; Data Privacy Act; DepEd records practices; CSC personnel record rules.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports compliance evidence and records maturity across all PRIME-HRM areas.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Retains competency, performance, L&D, promotion, and recognition evidence according to approved record rules.

## System Responsibilities

Maintain retention metadata, holds, disposition approvals, document history, access logs, and audit-ready reports.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - retention dates, queues, alerts, and reports are highly structured once rules are configured.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can assist classification suggestions, but retention decisions require verified records policy.

## AI Opportunities

Document classification suggestions, retention-risk summaries, duplicate record detection, and audit packet summaries.

## Reports Produced

Retention schedule, records due for review, disposition log, access log, compliance report.

## Security Considerations

Protect restricted records, prevent unauthorized deletion, log access, and require approvals for disposition.

## Future Integrations

Document Management, Digital Signature, Government Portals, AI Assistant.
