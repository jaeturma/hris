# 10 Employee Onboarding

## Purpose

Convert an approved appointment into a complete employee setup with assignment, account access, 201 file creation, orientation, and initial competency or development baseline.

## Trigger

Appointment approval, new hire reporting date, transfer-in, promotion appointment, or reemployment action.

## Actors

New employee, HR officer, records officer, office or school head, ICT administrator, payroll preparer, supervisor.

## Inputs

Appointment record, applicant record, plantilla item, position profile, required onboarding documents, assignment details, user account request, and orientation checklist.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Confirm appointment status and effective date.
2. Create or update employee master record.
3. Assign employee to office, station, position, item, supervisor, and work schedule where applicable.
4. Create 201 file index and attach verified documents.
5. Initiate orientation, account access, payroll preparation, and initial IDP or competency baseline.
6. Confirm onboarding completion and audit evidence.

## Decision Points

- Is the appointment final enough to create an employee record?
- Are required documents complete?
- Which office, station, role, and access scope applies?
- Is initial competency assessment or IDP required?

## Outputs

Employee record, assignment record, 201 file index, onboarding checklist, user access request, payroll preparation trigger, orientation completion record.

## Applicable Policy Area

DepEd appointment and personnel onboarding practices; CSC rules; Data Privacy Act; government records management; PRIME-HRM.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports Recruitment, Selection, and Placement plus onboarding evidence for HR maturity.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Links position profile to new employee, establishes baseline competencies, identifies initial gaps, and starts IDP planning.

## System Responsibilities

Prevent duplicate employee records, preserve applicant-to-employee traceability, enforce office scope, initialize document records, and log onboarding completion.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - checklist creation, profile conversion, access requests, and reminders are repeatable.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can summarize onboarding gaps but should not auto-create sensitive access or records without approval.

## AI Opportunities

Onboarding checklist assistant, missing document summaries, initial competency gap narrative, and first-week task reminders.

## Reports Produced

New hire onboarding tracker, incomplete onboarding report, account access pending list, initial IDP needed list.

## Security Considerations

Limit new employee access until verified; protect personal documents; audit account provisioning and document uploads.

## Future Integrations

Payroll, Document Management, Email, SMS, Digital Signature, Learning Management, Mobile Application, AI Assistant.

## Related Business Processes

Recruitment Selection Appointment, 201 Files Management, Personnel Movement, Learning Development, Competency Management.
