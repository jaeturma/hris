# 04 Recruitment Selection Appointment

## Purpose

Manage the general DepEd recruitment, selection, and appointment cycle from vacancy validation through publication, application, evaluation, HRMPSB action, recommendation, and appointment tracking.

This process is specifically for open-vacancy appointment and must not be used as a catch-all workflow for career progression, expanded reclassification, retitling, position conversion, Master Teacher progression, School Principal progression, or other personnel movement.

## Trigger

Approved vacancy, workforce plan, newly available plantilla item, replacement need, promotion vacancy, or office staffing directive.

## Actors

Applicant, HR officer, HRMPSB secretariat, evaluator, HRMPSB member, appointing authority, records officer, auditor.

## Inputs

Vacancy, publication, position profile, qualification standard, documentary requirements, applications, submitted documents, evaluation criteria, competency evidence, and HRMPSB records.

Required governance inputs include policy version, local memorandum version where applicable, Merit Selection Plan version, qualification-standard version, assessment-framework version, competency-framework version, HRMPSB constitution, effectivity period, and transition rule if the vacancy overlaps a policy change.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Validate vacancy and publication requirements.
2. Publish the vacancy and accept applications.
3. Capture applicant profile, applications, and submitted documents.
4. Check documentary completeness and qualification standards.
5. Conduct initial evaluation, comparative assessment, interview, or other required assessments.
6. Freeze immutable applicant and evaluation snapshots before HRMPSB action.
7. Route results to the cycle-specific or constitution-specific HRMPSB for deliberation and recommendation.
8. Generate ranking, CAR, CAR-RQA, RQA-style, or appointment recommendation outputs where applicable.
9. Track appointment preparation, appointing-authority action, CSC submission where required, CSC action, and records update.

## Decision Points

- Is the vacancy valid and approved for publication?
- Is the applicant eligible and documentarily complete?
- Which criteria, scoring, and ranking rules apply to the position?
- Which policy version, assessment framework, QS version, and MSP version apply on the relevant effectivity date?
- Is HRMPSB action complete, quorum-confirmed, conflict-checked, and properly recorded?
- Has appointment authority acted on the recommendation?
- Does the appointment require CSC submission, correction, validation, or tracking of returned action?

## Outputs

Publication notice, application records, QS results, evaluation results, interview records, HRMPSB record, ranking, comparative assessment output, appointment recommendation, and appointment tracker.

## Applicable Policy Area

DepEd recruitment, selection, appointment, ranking, and placement issuances; relevant CO, RO, and SDO memoranda; CSC rules and Qualification Standards; Data Privacy Act; records management.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Directly supports Recruitment, Selection, and Placement and provides evidence for meritocracy and excellence in government HRM.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Compares applicant qualifications and competencies against position profiles and preserves competency evidence for placement and future development.

## System Responsibilities

Support configurable criteria, document checklists, workflow states, evaluator assignments, HRMPSB permissions, ranking outputs, appointment tracking, and audit logs.

The system must preserve immutable snapshots of applications, submitted document metadata, QS checks, assessment frameworks, evaluation criteria, evaluator identities, scores, comments, HRMPSB membership, recusals, quorum, deliberation metadata, ranking output, appointment recommendation, and policy versions used.

## Workflow Boundary

Open-vacancy appointment begins with a published or authorized vacancy and leads to appointment recommendation and appointment action. It must not be used for:

- Promotion to higher teaching positions governed by a specialized promotion framework.
- Expanded reclassification under career progression rules.
- Retitling or position conversion actions.
- Master Teacher progression.
- School Principal progression.
- Transfer, reassignment, detail, or other personnel movement without open competition.

## Policy Versioning Requirements

- Link every cycle to a policy registry record and effectivity period.
- Link Regional and Division Memoranda as local implementing or clarifying issuances without overriding national policy improperly.
- Lock the applicable policy version at cycle opening, then document transition handling if policy changes before appointment.
- Do not hardcode scoring criteria, point allocations, documentary requirements, or ranking outputs.
- Flag unresolved policy conflicts for HR/legal validation before implementation.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

Very High - the process has repeatable checklists, routing, deadlines, scoring calculations, and report outputs after policy confirmation.

## AI Readiness Rating

High - AI can assist with document completeness, evidence summaries, and consistency checks while final evaluation remains human-led.

Applicant packet summaries, missing-document detection, QS evidence summaries, competency evidence extraction, and draft HRMPSB meeting packets.

## Reports Produced

## Security Considerations

Restrict applicant personal data, evaluator access, HRMPSB deliberation records, document viewing, exports, and recommendation actions.
