# 08 Career Progression

## Purpose

Manage expanded career progression for teachers and school heads using position levels, performance evidence, competency evidence, L&D history, and promotion readiness.

Career progression is a pathway family, not a single promotion workflow. It must distinguish Master Teacher progression, School Principal progression, expanded reclassification, retitling or position conversion, and any open-vacancy appointment connected to a progression level.

## Trigger

Career progression cycle, eligible employee application, new level opportunity, promotion track update, or policy-directed progression activity.

## Actors

Employee, HR officer, supervisor, school head, evaluator, HRMPSB, appointing authority, records officer.

## Inputs

Career progression levels, position profile, competency model, performance ratings, service record, L&D records, supporting documents, assessments, and promotion track.

Required governance inputs include career-progression policy version, pathway type, qualification-standard version, assessment-framework version, competency-framework version, historical performance-rating snapshots, transitory provision flag, and effectivity period.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Identify eligible employees and target career progression level.
2. Validate requirements, position availability, and policy configuration.
3. Collect performance, competency, service, and document evidence.
4. Classify the pathway: Master Teacher progression, School Principal progression, expanded reclassification, retitling or position conversion, open-vacancy appointment, or other authorized pathway.
5. Freeze employee, performance-rating, competency, service, and document snapshots.
6. Assess readiness and route for evaluation, HRMPSB action, or approval according to the pathway.
7. Record recommendation, approval, appointment, reclassification, retitling, conversion, or movement.
8. Update employee profile, service record, competency record, IDP, and plantilla or position records as applicable.

## Decision Points

- Is the employee eligible for the target progression level?
- Are performance and competency expectations met?
- Does the progression require appointment, reclassification, or movement?
- Does the pathway involve Master Teacher progression, School Principal progression, retitling, conversion, or expanded reclassification?
- Which policy version, transition rule, and effectivity period applies?
- What evidence must be retained for audit?

## Outputs

Career progression application, readiness assessment, recommendation, approval history, movement or appointment record, updated IDP.

## Applicable Policy Area

DepEd expanded career progression guidance and amendments; DepEd promotion and reclassification memoranda; CSC rules; PRIME-HRM; CBHRMS.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports Performance Management, L&D, Recruitment/Placement, and succession readiness.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Uses current proficiency, required proficiency, gap analysis, learning plan, promotion readiness, and succession readiness.

## System Responsibilities

Maintain career levels, eligibility statuses, evidence checklists, competency comparisons, approval history, and downstream personnel updates.

The system must preserve pathway classification, applicable policy version, assessment framework, competency framework, historical performance-rating snapshots, evidence-based competency assessments, HRMPSB governance records where required, and downstream appointment, CSC, reclassification, retitling, conversion, plantilla, movement, and service-record actions.

## Pathway Boundary

Career progression records must not collapse distinct actions into a generic promotion status. At minimum, the architecture must support:

- Master Teacher progression.
- School Principal progression.
- Expanded reclassification.
- Retitling or position conversion.
- Open-vacancy appointment linked to career progression.
- Other authorized career movement with separate policy basis.

## Transition and Transitory Provisions

Each career progression cycle must identify whether applications are governed by old rules, new rules, or a transitory rule. Pending applications at the time of a policy change must retain the originally applied framework unless HR/legal validation confirms a different transition rule.
