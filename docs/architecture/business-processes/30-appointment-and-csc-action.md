# 30 Appointment and CSC Action

## Purpose

Track appointment preparation, appointing-authority action, CSC submission where required, CSC action, corrections, resubmissions, and final personnel-record updates.

## Trigger

Appointment recommendation, promotion recommendation, career progression action, reclassification action requiring appointment or CSC validation, transfer-in, or corrected appointment document.

## Actors

HR officer, appointing authority, records officer, CSC liaison or authorized submitter, employee or appointee, auditor.

## Inputs

Recommendation record, selected applicant or employee snapshot, plantilla item, position profile, appointment documents, supporting documents, policy version, CSC submission requirements, returned action records.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Receive appointment recommendation or approved personnel action.
2. Validate item, appointee, policy version, documents, and appointing authority.
3. Prepare appointment packet and route for appointing-authority action.
4. Submit to CSC where required and track submission metadata.
5. Record CSC action, returned documents, correction, resubmission, or final acceptance.
6. Update employee record, service record, plantilla encumbrance, 201 file, and audit trail.

## Decision Points

- Is appointment required, or is the action reclassification, retitling, conversion, or movement without appointment?
- Is CSC submission required for this action type?
- Are returned actions correctable, resubmittable, or closed?
- Which effective date governs employee, plantilla, payroll, and service-record updates?

## Outputs

Appointment packet record, appointing-authority action, CSC submission tracker, CSC action record, correction history, service-record update, 201 file update.

## Applicable Policy Area

CSC appointment and other HR action rules; DepEd appointment guidance; relevant Regional and Division Memoranda; Data Privacy Act; records management.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports Recruitment, Selection, and Placement evidence and government appointment compliance.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Appointment packets should retain the competency and assessment snapshot that supported selection or progression.

## System Responsibilities

Track appointment lifecycle separately from selection. Preserve recommendation snapshots, appointment documents, CSC submission metadata, returned action history, and downstream updates.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - packet checklists, status tracking, reminders, and downstream update queues are automatable.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can summarize missing packet items and returned action history, but appointment decisions and CSC compliance require authorized review.

## AI Opportunities

Appointment packet completeness checks, returned-action summaries, service-record update suggestions, and status explanations.

## Reports Produced

Appointment tracker, CSC submission log, returned action report, pending corrections, completed appointment report.

## Security Considerations

Restrict appointment packets, personal data, CSC submission metadata, returned actions, and document exports.

## Future Integrations

Document Management, Digital Signature, Government Portals, Email, SMS, Payroll, AI Assistant.
