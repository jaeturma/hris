# 18 Performance Management

## Purpose

Support performance planning, monitoring, rating, review, and linkage to competency expectations, L&D, promotion readiness, and rewards.

## Trigger

Performance cycle opening, mid-year review, year-end rating, supervisor evaluation, promotion requirement, L&D planning, or recognition nomination.

## Actors

Employee, supervisor, school or office head, HR performance administrator, approving authority, auditor.

## Inputs

Employee assignment, performance targets, rating records, competency expectations, accomplishments, evidence documents, supervisor notes, and review approvals.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Establish performance cycle and employee scope.
2. Define targets and expectations according to role and office context.
3. Monitor progress and collect evidence.
4. Conduct rating and review.
5. Link performance results to competency gaps, IDP, promotion readiness, and rewards eligibility.
6. Preserve approved ratings and audit history.

## Decision Points

- Is the employee included in the cycle?
- Are targets and ratings approved?
- Do ratings trigger L&D, recognition, promotion readiness, or intervention?
- What information may be shown to HRMPSB or supervisors?

## Outputs

Performance plan, performance rating, review history, competency linkage, IDP update, rewards or promotion readiness evidence.

## Applicable Policy Area

SPMS-related requirements, CSC rules, PRIME-HRM Performance Management, DepEd performance guidance, Data Privacy Act.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Directly supports Performance Management and contributes to L&D, Rewards and Recognition, and merit-based personnel actions.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Connects performance ratings with competency expectations, gaps, learning plans, promotion readiness, and demonstrated competencies.

## System Responsibilities

Manage cycles, ratings, approvals, links to competencies and IDPs, evidence documents, restricted views, and audit trail.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - cycle setup, reminders, approvals, dashboards, and linkage reports are repeatable.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can summarize evidence and trends, but ratings and evaluative judgment must remain human-led.

## AI Opportunities

Performance evidence summaries, IDP suggestions from ratings, rating distribution summaries, and promotion evidence briefs.

## Reports Produced

Performance cycle report, rating distribution, pending ratings, competency-performance linkage report, IDP trigger report.

## Security Considerations

Performance ratings are highly sensitive; restrict access, exports, supervisor comments, and HRMPSB usage.

## Future Integrations

Learning Management, Document Management, Email, AI Assistant.

## Related Business Processes

Competency Management, Learning Development, Career Progression, Rewards Recognition, Discipline Grievance.
