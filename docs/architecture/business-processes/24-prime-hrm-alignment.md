# 24 PRIME-HRM Alignment

## Purpose

Coordinate the HRIS evidence, reports, process maturity indicators, and data quality controls needed to support CSC PRIME-HRM readiness.

## Trigger

PRIME-HRM assessment preparation, internal audit, HR maturity review, management request, policy update, or process improvement cycle.

## Actors

HR leadership, PRIME-HRM focal person, HR officers, records officer, L&D officer, performance administrator, rewards committee, auditor, system administrator.

## Inputs

Recruitment records, L&D plans, performance ratings, rewards records, competency data, audit logs, policy configurations, reports, and evidence documents.

Required governance inputs include policy registry, Merit Selection Plan versions, HRMPSB constitutions, competency-framework versions, assessment-framework versions, qualification-standard versions, performance-rating snapshots, and evidence indexes.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Map HRIS processes to PRIME-HRM areas.
2. Identify required evidence and responsible owners.
3. Confirm that evidence is linked to the correct policy version, effectivity period, and framework version.
4. Monitor completeness, timeliness, data quality, immutable snapshots, and auditability.
5. Generate readiness reports and evidence indexes.
6. Route gaps to process owners for correction, policy validation, or process improvement.
7. Preserve assessment history and management actions.

## Decision Points

- Which PRIME-HRM area is being reviewed?
- Is evidence complete, current, and traceable?
- Does the gap require data correction, process change, policy validation, or training?
- Who certifies readiness materials?
- Are HRMPSB, MSP, competency, performance, and assessment records versioned and auditable?

## Outputs

PRIME-HRM evidence index, readiness dashboard, gap register, corrective action tracker, management report.

## Applicable Policy Area

CSC PRIME-HRM; CSC CBHRMS direction; DepEd HR policy areas; Data Privacy Act; government records management.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Directly supports Recruitment, Selection, and Placement; Learning and Development; Performance Management; Rewards and Recognition; and HR systems maturity.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Uses competency model, assessment, IDP, performance linkage, promotion readiness, succession readiness, and rewards evidence.

## System Responsibilities

Tag records by PRIME-HRM area, produce evidence indexes, show readiness gaps, preserve audit trails, and avoid exposing sensitive evidence beyond authorized users.

The system must be able to show that recruitment, promotion, career progression, reclassification, L&D, performance, and rewards decisions used the correct policy version, MSP version, HRMPSB constitution, assessment framework, competency framework, QS version, and immutable evidence snapshots.

## PRIME-HRM Evidence Controls

- PRIME-HRM dashboards must separate evidence for Recruitment/Selection/Placement, L&D, Performance Management, and Rewards and Recognition.
- Evidence indexes must include policy version, effectivity period, owner, record source, sensitivity, and audit status.
- Local Regional and Division Memoranda may be linked as implementing or clarifying issuances but must not erase the national policy basis.
- Gaps involving policy interpretation, transitory provisions, or supersession must be flagged for manual HR/legal validation.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - evidence indexing, completeness checks, dashboards, and action trackers are repeatable.

## AI Readiness Rating

High - AI can summarize evidence and gaps, but official submissions need HR validation.

## AI Opportunities

Readiness narrative drafts, evidence summaries, gap clustering, corrective action suggestions, and management briefing drafts.

## Reports Produced

PRIME-HRM readiness dashboard, evidence index, gap register, corrective action report, area-level maturity summary.

## Security Considerations

Restrict evidence containing personal data, performance records, and committee actions; log exports and access.

## Future Integrations

Document Management, Government Portals, Email, Digital Signature, AI Assistant.

## Related Business Processes

Recruitment Selection Appointment, Learning Development, Performance Management, Rewards Recognition, Competency Management, Records Retention.
