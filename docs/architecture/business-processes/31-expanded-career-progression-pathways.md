# 31 Expanded Career Progression Pathways

## Purpose

Define the pathway architecture that separates career progression, Master Teacher progression, School Principal progression, promotion to higher teaching positions, expanded reclassification, retitling, position conversion, open-vacancy appointment, and other personnel movement.

## Trigger

Career progression cycle, higher teaching position vacancy, Master Teacher pathway, School Principal pathway, expanded reclassification request, retitling or conversion action, or policy transition.

## Actors

Employee, HR officer, supervisor, school head, competency administrator, evaluator, HRMPSB, appointing authority, plantilla manager, records officer.

## Inputs

Pathway type, policy version, effectivity period, transitory provision, position profile, competency framework, QS version, assessment framework, performance-rating snapshots, service record, L&D evidence, documents.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Identify employee intent, office need, or policy-triggered pathway.
2. Classify the action into the correct pathway before collecting evidence.
3. Link pathway to policy version, QS version, competency framework, assessment framework, MSP version, and HRMPSB governance where required.
4. Freeze employee, performance, competency, service, and document snapshots.
5. Evaluate through the pathway-specific framework.
6. Route to HRMPSB, approving authority, appointment, CSC action, reclassification, retitling, conversion, or movement process as applicable.
7. Update employee, position, plantilla, service, competency, and records data according to the approved action.

## Decision Points

- Is the pathway open-vacancy appointment, promotion, expanded reclassification, career progression, retitling, conversion, Master Teacher progression, School Principal progression, or movement?
- Does the action require HRMPSB evaluation, appointment, CSC submission, plantilla update, or service-record entry?
- Which transition rule applies if policy changed during the cycle?
- Which evidence is required and which snapshot must be preserved?

## Outputs

Pathway record, evidence snapshot, evaluation result, HRMPSB recommendation where applicable, approved action, appointment or CSC tracker where applicable, updated employee and position records.

## Applicable Policy Area

DepEd Orders on hiring, higher teaching positions, expanded career progression, amendments and clarifications, CSC appointment and HR action rules, PRIME-HRM, CBHRMS, Regional and Division Memoranda.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports Recruitment/Selection/Placement, Performance Management, L&D, Rewards and Recognition, succession readiness, and strategic HR maturity.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Every pathway should compare required competencies, current evidence, competency gaps, learning history, promotion readiness, and succession readiness using the applicable framework version.

## System Responsibilities

Provide a pathway classifier, versioned configuration links, immutable snapshots, action-specific downstream updates, and clear separation from ordinary personnel movement.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - pathway classification checks, evidence checklists, version locks, and downstream task creation can be automated after policy validation.

## AI Readiness Rating

High - AI can summarize pathway evidence and gaps, but classification and final action must be validated by HR.

## AI Opportunities

Pathway selection assistant, evidence gap summaries, transition warnings, readiness narratives, and downstream action checklists.

## Reports Produced

Career pathway pipeline, Master Teacher progression report, School Principal progression report, expanded reclassification tracker, retitling/conversion tracker, transition impact report.

## Security Considerations

Protect performance, competency, promotion, reclassification, and HRMPSB data; log all pathway classification changes and evidence access.

## Future Integrations

Document Management, Digital Signature, Government Portals, Learning Management, Payroll, AI Assistant.

## Related Business Processes

Career Progression, Reclassification, School Administrator Promotion, Competency Management, Performance Management, Appointment and CSC Action, Personnel Movement.
