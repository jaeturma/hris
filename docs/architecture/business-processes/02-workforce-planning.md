# 02 Workforce Planning

## Purpose

Plan staffing requirements across Region, Division, District, School, Implementing Unit, and Office / Unit based on plantilla, vacancies, school needs, competencies, and projected personnel movement.

## Trigger

Annual planning, new school or office needs, vacancies, retirements, transfers, enrollment or workload changes, policy directives, or competency gaps.

## Actors

Regional HR, Division HR, planning office, school head, office head, plantilla manager, budget personnel, approving authority.

## Inputs

Current plantilla, filled and vacant items, employee assignments, retirement forecasts, competency profiles, service records, performance trends, and school or office staffing requests.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Collect current workforce and plantilla inventory.
2. Identify vacancies, surplus, hard-to-fill roles, and retirement risks.
3. Compare current workforce competencies against position and office needs.
4. Prioritize hiring, deployment, L&D, promotion, or reclassification actions.
5. Route plan for review and approval.
6. Use approved plan to initiate vacancy, movement, L&D, or career progression processes.

## Decision Points

- Is the need a hiring, transfer, L&D, promotion, reclassification, or workload issue?
- Does an authorized item exist?
- Which office owns the planning decision?
- Are competencies available internally or should recruitment be initiated?

## Outputs

Workforce plan, vacancy forecast, retirement forecast, competency gap summary, priority staffing list, and implementation tracker.

## Applicable Policy Area

DepEd staffing and personnel action guidance; CSC rules; PRIME-HRM strategic HR planning; CBHRMS; data privacy and records requirements.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports strategic Recruitment, Selection, and Placement plus L&D and succession readiness evidence.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Uses required competencies, current competency inventory, gap analysis, IDP needs, promotion readiness, and succession readiness.

## System Responsibilities

Provide planning dashboards, position and employee counts, office-scope filtering, vacancy indicators, competency heatmaps, and auditable planning decisions.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - staffing inventories, forecasts, vacancy detection, and dashboards can be automated from structured records.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can summarize patterns, but workforce planning recommendations require validated local context and human approval.

## AI Opportunities

Draft workforce risk summaries, detect recurring shortage areas, summarize competency gaps by office, and flag retirement-driven vacancy risks.

## Reports Produced

Workforce plan, vacancy projection, staffing gap report, competency gap report, retirement forecast, and office staffing dashboard.

## Security Considerations

Restrict workforce planning data by office scope and role; protect personal data and retirement-related information.

## Future Integrations

Payroll, Government Portals, Learning Management, AI Assistant, Document Management.

## Related Business Processes

Plantilla Management, Recruitment Selection Appointment, Career Progression, Learning Development, Competency Management, Separation Retirement.
