# 05 Teacher Hiring

## Purpose

Support DepEd teacher hiring workflows, including application, screening, comparative assessment, ranking, registry-style outputs where applicable, appointment recommendation, and placement.

## Trigger

Teacher vacancy, school staffing need, teacher registry cycle, newly authorized item, replacement need, or policy-directed hiring period.

## Actors

Teacher-applicant, school or SDO HR, evaluator, HRMPSB, school head, appointing authority, records officer.

## Inputs

Teacher vacancy, publication, applicant profile, education, eligibility, experience, training, performance or demonstration evidence where applicable, documents, criteria, and position competency profile.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Validate teacher position and publication details.
2. Accept teacher applications and documentary requirements.
3. Conduct QS and completeness checks.
4. Conduct initial evaluation and comparative assessment using verified criteria.
5. Record HRMPSB evaluation and ranking.
6. Produce CAR, CAR-RQA, RQA-style, or equivalent outputs where applicable.
7. Track appointment recommendation and placement to school or office.

## Decision Points

- Which teacher hiring issuance or memorandum applies?
- Is the applicant eligible and within the correct registry or vacancy context?
- Which criteria, cutoffs, ranking, or registry output applies?
- Is the target school, district, or division placement valid?

## Outputs

Teacher applicant records, document checklist, evaluation result, ranking, CAR / CAR-RQA / RQA-style output where applicable, recommendation, appointment tracker, and placement record.

## Applicable Policy Area

DepEd teacher hiring, placement, ranking, and appointment issuances; relevant CO, RO, and SDO memoranda; CSC rules; Data Privacy Act; records management.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports Recruitment, Selection, and Placement with merit-based evidence and transparent ranking records.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Teacher competencies should connect to position profiles, classroom-related evidence, competency gaps, L&D needs, and promotion readiness.

## System Responsibilities

Provide teacher-specific cycles, configurable criteria, document lists, registry-style output templates, school placement tracking, and audit logs.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

Very High - high-volume teacher hiring benefits strongly from checklist validation, ranking support, and report generation.

## AI Readiness Rating

High - AI can summarize applicant packets and detect missing evidence, with human evaluators retaining authority.

## AI Opportunities

Applicant evidence summaries, duplicate application checks, missing document detection, ranking packet preparation, and appointment status explanations.

## Reports Produced

Teacher applicant list, qualified applicant list, ranking report, registry-style report, appointment recommendation list, and school placement report.

## Security Considerations

Protect applicant data, evaluator records, ranking results before release, document access, and export history.

## Future Integrations

Document Management, Email, SMS, Digital Signature, Government Portals, Mobile Application, AI Assistant.

## Related Business Processes

Recruitment Selection Appointment, Workforce Planning, Plantilla Management, Employee Onboarding, Competency Management.
