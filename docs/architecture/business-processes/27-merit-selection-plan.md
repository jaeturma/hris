# 27 Merit Selection Plan

## Purpose

Manage versioned Merit Selection Plan (MSP) records that govern merit-based recruitment, selection, placement, promotion, and HRMPSB participation.

## Trigger

New MSP, amended MSP, CSC appointment rule update, DepEd policy change, HRMPSB process update, or local implementing guidance.

## Actors

HR leadership, HR policy owner, HRMPSB secretariat, approving authority, legal or policy reviewer, auditor.

## Inputs

MSP version, effectivity period, covered positions, selection principles, HRMPSB governance rules, appointment pathways, assessment-framework links, and policy registry references.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Register MSP version and applicable policy basis.
2. Identify covered appointment and selection pathways.
3. Link MSP version to HRMPSB constitutions, assessment frameworks, QS versions, and report templates.
4. Approve MSP configuration for future cycles.
5. Lock MSP version when a recruitment, promotion, or progression cycle opens.
6. Preserve historical MSP versions for completed evaluations.

## Decision Points

- Which positions and appointment pathways are covered?
- Does a specialized HRMPSB apply?
- Which version applies to pending cycles during transition?
- Are local memoranda implementing or improperly overriding national policy?

## Outputs

MSP version record, coverage matrix, pathway map, HRMPSB governance linkage, assessment linkage, transition notes.

## Applicable Policy Area

CSC appointment and HR action rules including Merit Selection Plan and HRMPSB requirements; DepEd recruitment, promotion, placement, and career progression issuances; Regional and Division Memoranda.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Directly supports Recruitment, Selection, and Placement and strengthens meritocracy evidence.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

MSP versions should define where competency-based evidence is used in selection, placement, promotion, progression, and succession readiness.

## System Responsibilities

Version MSP records, prevent cycles without an applicable MSP, link MSP to HRMPSB and assessment frameworks, and preserve the MSP snapshot used in completed evaluations.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - coverage checks, missing MSP warnings, cycle locking, and transition alerts are automatable.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can summarize MSP coverage, but interpretation and approval require HR/legal validation.

## AI Opportunities

MSP coverage summaries, pathway checklists, transition risk detection, and cycle setup assistance.

## Reports Produced

MSP version register, covered pathway matrix, pending cycles by MSP version, MSP validation gap report.

## Security Considerations

Restrict MSP configuration approval; log changes; prevent silent edits to versions used by completed cycles.

## Future Integrations

Document Management, Digital Signature, Government Portals, AI Assistant.

## Related Business Processes

Policy Versioning, HRMPSB Governance, Assessment Framework Engine, Recruitment Selection Appointment, Career Progression.
