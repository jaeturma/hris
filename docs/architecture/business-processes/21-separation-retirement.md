# 21 Separation Retirement

## Purpose

Manage resignation, retirement, death, transfer-out, end of appointment, or other separation actions with clearance, benefits preparation, service record closure, and retention.

## Trigger

Retirement eligibility, resignation notice, death report, transfer-out, termination of appointment, separation approval, or workforce planning forecast.

## Actors

Employee or representative, HR officer, supervisor, school or office head, records officer, payroll preparer, approving authority.

## Inputs

Employee record, service record, separation type, effective date, clearance records, leave balances, documents, benefits-related data, and approval record.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Initiate separation or retirement record.
2. Validate separation type, effective date, required documents, and approvals.
3. Route clearance and office accountability checks.
4. Prepare service record, leave, payroll, and records updates.
5. Close or change user access and office assignment.
6. Apply retention and archival rules.

## Decision Points

- What separation type applies?
- Are clearance, documents, and approvals complete?
- Does the action affect leave balances, payroll preparation, service record, or benefits documents?
- What access should be revoked or retained?

## Outputs

Separation record, retirement tracker, clearance status, final service record, leave balance summary, access deactivation record, retention action.

## Applicable Policy Area

CSC rules, DepEd personnel separation practices, records management, Data Privacy Act, payroll and benefits coordination requirements.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports workforce planning, records integrity, and employee lifecycle management.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Separation data informs succession readiness, workforce planning, and competency vacancy risks.

## System Responsibilities

Track separation status, clearances, effective dates, service record closure, access changes, document retention, and audit history.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - clearance routing, reminders, service record queue, and access deactivation can be automated.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can summarize clearance and records readiness, but sensitive separation handling requires human control.

## AI Opportunities

Retirement forecast summaries, clearance missing-item summaries, service record readiness checks, and succession vacancy alerts.

## Reports Produced

Retirement forecast, separation tracker, clearance report, final records checklist, access deactivation report.

## Security Considerations

Protect personal, benefits, health, and family-related records; promptly adjust system access after effective date.

## Future Integrations

Payroll, Government Portals, Document Management, Email, Digital Signature, AI Assistant.

Workforce Planning, Service Record Management, Records Retention, Personnel Movement, Plantilla Management.
