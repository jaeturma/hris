# 28 HRMPSB Governance

## Purpose

Define HRMPSB constitution, specialized HRMPSBs, cycle-specific membership, quorum, conflict-of-interest declarations, recusals, deliberation metadata, and recommendation records.

## Trigger

Recruitment cycle, teacher hiring cycle, promotion cycle, career progression pathway, reclassification action requiring board review, or HRMPSB constitution update.

## Actors

HRMPSB chair, members, alternate members, secretariat, evaluator, appointing authority, HR policy owner, auditor.

## Inputs

HRMPSB constitution, membership list, roles, terms or cycle coverage, specialization, conflict declarations, recusal records, quorum rules, meeting records, recommendation outputs.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Register HRMPSB constitution or specialized HRMPSB.
2. Assign members, alternates, secretariat, coverage, and effectivity.
3. Link constitution to policy version, MSP version, and cycle.
4. Collect conflict-of-interest and recusal declarations.
5. Confirm quorum and authorized participation before deliberation.
6. Record deliberation metadata, recommendation, dissent or abstention metadata where applicable, and audit trail.

## Decision Points

- Is the HRMPSB regular or specialized?
- Is membership constitution-specific or cycle-specific?
- Are conflicts or recusals present?
- Is quorum satisfied before deliberation?
- Are members authorized for the pathway and office scope?

## Outputs

HRMPSB constitution record, membership snapshot, conflict declarations, recusal log, quorum record, deliberation metadata, recommendation record.

## Applicable Policy Area

CSC appointment and HR action rules on MSP and HRMPSB; DepEd hiring, promotion, and appointment issuances; Regional and Division Memoranda; Data Privacy Act.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports merit-based Recruitment, Selection, and Placement and audit-ready HR governance.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

HRMPSB may review competency evidence, position competency profiles, leadership readiness, and assessment outputs depending on the pathway.

## System Responsibilities

Represent HRMPSB membership as records, not only roles. Preserve membership snapshots, recusals, quorum, deliberation metadata, and recommendations for each cycle.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - membership checks, conflict declarations, quorum validation, and packet routing are automatable.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can summarize packets and decisions, but deliberation and recommendations remain human functions.

## AI Opportunities

Board packet summaries, missing declaration alerts, quorum readiness checks, and recommendation packet indexing.

## Reports Produced

HRMPSB constitution register, cycle membership report, conflict and recusal report, quorum report, recommendation register.

## Security Considerations

Protect deliberation records, evaluator comments, conflict declarations, and unpublished recommendations. Log all access.

## Future Integrations

Document Management, Email, Digital Signature, AI Assistant.

## Related Business Processes

Recruitment Selection Appointment, Teacher Hiring, School Administrator Promotion, Career Progression, Assessment Framework Engine.
