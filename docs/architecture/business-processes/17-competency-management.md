# 17 Competency Management

## Purpose

Maintain competency models, competency dictionary, job families, proficiency levels, behavioral indicators, position profiles, assessments, gaps, and development links.

## Trigger

New competency model, updated position profile, assessment cycle, recruitment cycle, performance review, L&D plan, promotion, reclassification, or succession planning.

## Actors

Competency administrator, HR officer, supervisor, employee, evaluator, HRMPSB, approving authority.

## Inputs

Competency model, core competencies, leadership competencies, functional / technical competencies, proficiency levels, behavioral indicators, position competency profiles, assessment evidence.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Define or update competency model and dictionary.
2. Map competencies to job families and position profiles.
3. Assess employee or applicant competencies using approved methods.
4. Compare required and current proficiency levels.
5. Generate gap analysis and IDP links.
6. Use competency evidence in recruitment, placement, performance, promotion, reclassification, rewards, and succession readiness.

## Decision Points

- Is the competency model approved and versioned?
- Which competencies apply to the position or job family?
- What assessment method and evidence is valid?
- Which gaps require L&D, coaching, placement action, or promotion deferral?

## Outputs

Competency dictionary, position competency profile, assessment result, gap analysis, IDP linkage, readiness summary.

## Applicable Policy Area

CSC PRIME-HRM; CSC Resolution No. 2600005; Agency CBHRMS; DepEd hiring, promotion, and reclassification guidance; Data Privacy Act.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Strengthens all PRIME-HRM areas, especially Recruitment/Placement, L&D, Performance Management, and Rewards and Recognition.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

This is the core process for required/current competency comparison, gap analysis, learning plans, promotion readiness, and succession readiness.

## System Responsibilities

Version competency models, link profiles to positions, store assessments, compute gaps, protect evidence, and expose competency references across workflows.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - mapping, comparison, gap detection, and reports are structured once the model is approved.

## AI Readiness Rating

High - AI can summarize evidence and suggest development actions, subject to human review and approved competency definitions.

## AI Opportunities

Competency evidence summaries, behavioral indicator matching, gap narrative generation, IDP suggestions, and readiness dashboard explanations.

## Reports Produced

Competency dictionary, position profile matrix, competency assessment report, gap analysis report, succession readiness report.

## Security Considerations

Protect assessment evidence, supervisor comments, performance-linked competency records, and employee self-service boundaries.

## Future Integrations

Learning Management, Document Management, AI Assistant, Mobile Application.

## Related Business Processes

Recruitment Selection Appointment, Learning Development, Performance Management, Career Progression, Rewards Recognition.
