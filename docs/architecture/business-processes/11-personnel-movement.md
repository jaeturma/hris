# 11 Personnel Movement

## Purpose

Track employee movement such as transfer, reassignment, detail, promotion movement, return to station, or office reassignment while preserving service and assignment history.

## Trigger

Approved movement order, appointment, office need, employee request, promotion, reclassification, transfer-in, transfer-out, or separation preparation.

## Actors

Employee, HR officer, releasing office, receiving office, school head, approving authority, records officer.

## Inputs

Employee record, current assignment, target office or station, plantilla item, movement reason, effective dates, supporting documents, and approval record.

## High-Level Process Flow

1. Initiate movement request or receive approved movement document.
2. Validate current assignment, target assignment, item impact, and effective dates.
3. Route to required releasing, receiving, and approving authorities.
4. Record approval and implement assignment changes.
5. Update service record, 201 file, office scope, and reporting structures.
6. Preserve previous assignment history.

## Decision Points

- Is the movement temporary or permanent?
- Does the movement affect plantilla encumbrance, payroll preparation, or office scope?
- Are releasing and receiving approvals required?
- What service record entry is needed?

## Outputs

Movement record, updated assignment, assignment history, service record entry, document history, and office scope update.

## Applicable Policy Area

DepEd personnel action and movement guidance; CSC rules; Data Privacy Act; government records management.

## PRIME-HRM Contribution

Supports Recruitment/Placement, workforce responsiveness, and records accuracy.

## Competency-Based HRM Link

Placement should consider position competency requirements, current competencies, gaps, and development needs.

## System Responsibilities

Maintain effective-dated assignment history, movement types, office scope updates, approval history, and service record synchronization.

## Automation Opportunity Rating

High - routing, effective-date reminders, service record draft updates, and access scope changes can be automated.

## AI Readiness Rating

Medium - AI can flag inconsistencies and draft summaries, but final movement validation is policy and authority driven.

## AI Opportunities

Movement impact summary, service record draft suggestions, office scope change alerts, and missing approval detection.

## Reports Produced

Movement registry, transfer-in/out report, reassignment report, pending movement approvals, service record update queue.

## Security Considerations

Restrict movement approval, protect employee records, and update access scope promptly after effective date.

## Future Integrations

Payroll, Attendance Devices, Document Management, Email, Digital Signature, Government Portals, AI Assistant.
