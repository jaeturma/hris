# ADR-009 Approved Knowledge Sources Are Required for Policy Assistance

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Policy assistance must use approved and active knowledge sources by default. Superseded sources may be used only for historical transactions with clear labeling.

## Rationale

AI or search assistance must not invent or rely on unverified policy guidance.

## Consequences

Knowledge sources need statuses, versions, citations, and retrieval-scope controls.
