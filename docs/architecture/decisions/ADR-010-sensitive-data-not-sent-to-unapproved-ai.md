# ADR-010 Sensitive Data Cannot Be Sent to Unapproved AI Providers

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Sensitive HR records must not be sent to public or unapproved AI services.

## Rationale

Applicant, employee, medical, performance, disciplinary, and appointment data are sensitive government records.

## Consequences

AI integrations require provider approval, data minimization, contractual controls, training opt-out, audit rights, and exit strategy.
