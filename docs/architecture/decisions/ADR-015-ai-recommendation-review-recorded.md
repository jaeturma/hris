# ADR-015 AI Recommendation Acceptance or Rejection Is Recorded

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Human review of AI recommendations must record accepted, partially accepted, rejected, expired, superseded, invalidated, or requires-more-data states with reasons.

## Rationale

Decision support must be auditable and safe to reject.

## Consequences

Rejecting AI output is normal workflow behavior and must not be treated as user error.
