# ADR-016 PRIME-HRM Analytics Indicate Readiness but Do Not Replace Official Assessment

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

PRIME-HRM analytics provide readiness indicators, evidence completeness, and risk summaries but do not replace official CSC or DepEd assessment.

## Rationale

Analytics can support preparation, not certify official compliance by itself.

## Consequences

Dashboards must label readiness indicators, source evidence, limitations, and required human validation.
