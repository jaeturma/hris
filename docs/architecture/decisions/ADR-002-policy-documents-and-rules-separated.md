# ADR-002 Policy Documents and Executable Rules Are Separate but Linked

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Uploaded policy documents are authoritative references after verification, while executable rules are structured configurations linked to approved policy versions.

## Rationale

A PDF or memorandum upload alone cannot safely drive transactions. Structured rules need review, testing, and activation.

## Consequences

Policy sources, rule sets, QS versions, assessment frameworks, and workflow mappings require separate versioning and audit.
