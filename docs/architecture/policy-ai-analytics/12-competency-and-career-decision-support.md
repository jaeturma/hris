# 12 Competency and Career Decision Support

## Purpose

Define advisory support for competency, career progression, promotion readiness, succession readiness, and L&D planning.

## Supported Use Cases

Competency matching, competency gap explanation, IDP recommendations, learning intervention suggestions, promotion-readiness indicators, succession-readiness indicators, reclassification evidence checklist, Master Teacher pathway support, School Principal pathway support, and career-stage comparison.

## Controls

- Use verified competency framework versions and position competency profile versions.
- Use historical performance-rating snapshots.
- Show required, current, missing, and unverifiable evidence separately.
- Do not merge career progression, reclassification, retitling, and promotion into one generic pathway.
- Recommendations remain advisory and require authorized HR/supervisor/HRMPSB review where applicable.

## Outputs

Readiness summary, evidence gap list, development suggestion, policy/rule references, limitations, confidence/reliability indicator, and required human reviewer.
