# 08 Policy Exceptions and Overrides

## Purpose

Define controlled handling for exceptions, waivers, substitutions, equivalencies, temporary authorities, emergency procedures, and overrides.

## Exception Types

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Built-in exception | Explicitly authorized by policy. |
| Equivalency | Alternate evidence satisfies a requirement. |
| Substitution | One requirement substituted as policy permits. |
| Administrative waiver | Authorized waiver with legal/policy basis. |
| Temporary authority | Time-bound authority or directive. |
| Emergency procedure | Approved exceptional process. |
| Legal directive | Court/CSC/DepEd directive. |
| Data correction | Correction of wrong source data. |
| System override | Technical correction, not business authority. |
| Unauthorized bypass | Invalid and reportable attempt. |

## Required Exception Record

Policy/rule affected, applicant or employee affected, transaction, reason, legal/policy basis, supporting document, requesting user, reviewing authority, approving authority, effective period, scope, conditions, expiration, and audit trail.

## Authority Rule

System administrators do not automatically have authority to approve HR policy exceptions. Technical access and business authority remain separate.

## Override Controls

All overrides require permission, segregation-of-duties check, supporting basis, approval, expiration when applicable, immutable audit record, and review reporting.
