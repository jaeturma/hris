# 10 AI Use Cases and Prohibited Actions

## Permitted AI Capabilities

| Capability | Conditions |
| --- | --- |
| Document completeness summary | Uses authorized document metadata and shows missing evidence for human review. |
| OCR-assisted extraction | Extracted data requires human verification before structured update. |
| Policy retrieval assistance | Uses approved active sources and cites policy version. |
| Applicant packet summary | No scoring or disqualification; assigned HR/evaluator only. |
| Competency gap narrative | Based on verified competency assessments. |
| IDP suggestion | Advisory and supervisor/HR-reviewed. |
| Promotion-readiness indicator | Advisory; shows evidence, gaps, and limitations. |
| Workforce risk summary | Aggregated where possible; no hidden individual adverse action. |
| PRIME-HRM evidence summary | Evidence index and readiness narrative. |
| Dashboard narrative | Explains aggregate trends and data limitations. |

## Prohibited AI Actions

AI must not appoint, promote, reclassify, reject applicants, disqualify applicants without human action, issue official ranking, alter evaluator score, approve personnel action, sign appointments or evaluations, replace HRMPSB deliberation, replace appointing authority, override CSC requirements, modify official records without attribution, dispose government records, or make final disciplinary findings.

## Human Review Requirements

High-impact AI outputs require reviewer, decision, reason, supporting evidence, policy basis, conflicts identified, corrections, date/time, acting capacity, and office context.
