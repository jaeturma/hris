# 17 Explainability and Human Validation

## Purpose

Define explainability standards for advisory recommendations and deterministic rule results.

## Required Explanation Questions

What was recommended? Why was it recommended? What data was used? What policies were considered? What rules were triggered? What data was missing? What limitations apply? What confidence or reliability level applies? Who must review it? What official action is still required?

## Human Validation Record

Reviewer, decision, reason, supporting evidence, policy basis, conflicts identified, corrections, date/time, acting capacity, office context, and subsequent action.

## Model Card Requirements

For machine-learning models, document intended use, prohibited use, training-data description, evaluation metrics, bias testing, known limitations, version history, approval status, monitoring requirements, and retirement criteria.

## Rule

Avoid unexplained black-box scores for high-impact HR decisions. Official scores must come from deterministic frameworks, not generative AI.
