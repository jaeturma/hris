# 14 PRIME-HRM and Compliance Analytics

## Purpose

Support PRIME-HRM readiness, compliance monitoring, audit risk detection, and evidence indexing.

## Analytics Areas

Recruitment/Selection/Placement evidence, L&D evidence, Performance Management evidence, Rewards and Recognition evidence, policy compliance, HRMPSB governance, document completeness, retention status, access reviews, segregation-of-duties findings, and corrective actions.

## Rules

- PRIME-HRM analytics indicate readiness and do not replace official assessment.
- Evidence records must reference source domain, policy version, document version, office owner, sensitivity, and audit status.
- Compliance risks generate review tasks, not automatic adverse findings.
- Restricted evidence requires explicit permission and purpose-bound access.

## Outputs

Readiness dashboards, evidence completeness reports, compliance-risk flags, corrective-action trackers, audit-preparation summaries, and management briefings.
