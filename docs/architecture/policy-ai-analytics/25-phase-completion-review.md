## Cross-Phase Inconsistencies

No intentional contradiction was introduced. The Phase 0.10 architecture strengthens earlier decisions: policy rules stay in Policy and Configuration, analytics remain read-oriented, documents remain distinct from structured data, and AI remains advisory.

## Remaining Architecture Gaps

- Exact DepEd/CSC rule content must still be manually configured after official validation.
- Retention schedules require records-management validation.
- AI provider approval and deployment model remain procurement/security decisions.
- Metric definitions need HR leadership approval.

## Dependencies for Phase 1

Core Platform, Organization, Policy Registry, Document metadata foundation, office scope, role/permission model, audit foundation, and policy-source storage.

## Management Decision Risks

AI deployment boundary, external provider use, dashboard drill-down permissions, data retention policy, who can approve policy exceptions, and who owns rule configuration.

## Phase 0.11 Items

Enterprise architecture review, policy traceability matrix, implementation readiness checklist, cross-phase consistency audit, Phase 1 backlog, and validation plan with HR/legal/records/privacy stakeholders.
