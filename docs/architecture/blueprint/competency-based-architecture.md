# Competency-Based Architecture

Competency management is a foundation of the HRIS, not a future add-on.

## Purpose

Support CBHRMS, PRIME-HRM readiness, merit selection, SPMS alignment, PRAISE alignment, and strategic HR decisions.

## Actors

Competency administrator, HR planner, HR staff, supervisor, evaluator, HRMPSB member, employee, approving authority, auditor.

## Required Records

Competency Model, Competency Dictionary, proficiency level, behavioral indicator, job family, Position Competency Profile, Competency Assessment, competency evidence, gap analysis, Individual Development Plan, Learning and Development intervention, Performance Rating, promotion readiness, succession readiness, rewards evidence.

## Required Approvals

Competency dictionary approval, position profile approval, assessment validation where required, IDP approval, promotion or succession readiness review, rewards approval.

## Outputs

Position competency profiles, competency maps, assessment results, gap analysis, IDP, L&D plan, readiness reports, PRIME-HRM readiness evidence.

## Audit Trail

Log changes to competency definitions, mappings, assessments, evidence, IDPs, readiness indicators, and reports.

## Permissions

Competency definitions may be broadly readable, but assessments, gaps, IDPs, readiness indicators, and rewards evidence are sensitive and must be permission-gated.

## Policy Dependencies

CSC PRIME-HRM, CSC Resolution No. 2600005 on CBHRMS, SPMS alignment, PRAISE alignment, DepEd career progression and promotion guidance.

## Future Automation

Competency gap analysis, recommended L&D interventions, promotion readiness summaries, succession dashboards, and AI-assisted evidence summaries with human validation.
