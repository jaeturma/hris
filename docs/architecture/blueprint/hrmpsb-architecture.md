# HRMPSB Architecture

The HRMPSB architecture supports structured board participation, evaluation, recommendation, and audit evidence.

## Purpose

Provide a controlled workspace for HRMPSB-related evaluation records without turning deliberation support into an automated decision maker.

## Actors

HRMPSB chair, HRMPSB member, secretariat, HR staff, evaluator, approving authority, auditor.

## Required Records

HRMPSB membership, evaluator assignments, vacancy, application, documentary requirements, qualification standard result, evaluation criteria, assessment, interview, ranking, comparative assessment result, recommendation, audit log.

## Required Approvals

Board review, recommendation approval or certification, and appointing authority action where applicable.

## Outputs

Evaluation sheets, interview records, comparative assessment result, ranking list, recommendation record, board packet, audit report.

## Audit Trail

Log member access, assessment submission, score or criteria changes, ranking generation, recommendation, exports, and corrections.

## Permissions

Limit HRMPSB records to assigned members, secretariat, authorized HR staff, approving authorities, and auditors. Hide unrelated applicant personal data where not needed.

## Policy Dependencies

DepEd HRMPSB-related hiring, ranking, promotion, and appointment guidance; CSC merit and qualification standards; Data Privacy Act.

## Future Automation

Meeting packets, conflict checks, missing score reminders, comparative summaries, and decision-support notes with human review.
