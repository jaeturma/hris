# System Overview

The HRIS is a DepEd-first, PRIME-HRM-aligned, competency-based human resource information system for Regional Offices, Schools Division Offices, Implementing Units, and Large Schools.

DepEd hiring, placement, promotion, appointment, reclassification, and personnel action issuances are the primary implementation guides. CSC PRIME-HRM and CBHRMS strengthen the strategic HR layer by adding competency evidence, maturity readiness, performance linkage, learning and development, rewards and recognition, and meritocracy controls.

## Architectural Foundations

- DepEd office hierarchy: Region, Division, District, School, Implementing Unit, and Office / Station.
- DepEd recruitment, selection, appointment, promotion, reclassification, and personnel movement workflows.
- HRMPSB-assisted evaluation, ranking, and recommendation processes.
- Position, Plantilla Item, Item Number, Position Title, and Salary Grade management.
- Competency Model, Competency Dictionary, Position Competency Profile, Competency Assessment, and Individual Development Plan.
- Employee profile, 201 File Document, Service Record, Performance Rating, and Personnel Movement history.
- Spatie Permission RBAC, office scoping, audit trail, document history, and data privacy controls.

## System Character

The system must support both compliance and strategic HR management. DepEd Orders and Memoranda drive workflow logic; CSC PRIME-HRM and CBHRMS provide the competency-based and maturity-readiness layer.
