# Office Hierarchy

The HRIS must support DepEd governance and operational scope.

## Levels

- Region
- Schools Division Office
- District
- School
- Implementing Unit / Large School
- Office / Station

## Architecture Rules

- Every applicant, employee, vacancy, plantilla item, appointment, personnel movement, service record, document, and report must have clear office scope.
- Regional users may need cross-division oversight.
- Division users may manage division office and school-level processes.
- School and implementing-unit users must be restricted to their assigned scope.
- Office / Station must support assignment and service record reporting.

## Reporting Scope

Reports must be able to aggregate upward from School to District to Division to Region while preserving row-level privacy permissions.
