# Promotion and Reclassification Architecture

This architecture covers promotion to higher teaching positions, expanded career progression, reclassification requests, succession readiness, and personnel movement effects.

## Purpose

Support DepEd policy-guided promotion and reclassification processes while linking performance, competency evidence, position profiles, career progression levels, service history, and appointment or personnel movement outcomes.

## Actors

Employee-applicant, school or office head, HR staff, evaluator, HRMPSB member, approving authority, appointing authority, records officer, auditor.

## Required Records

Employee, Position, Position Title, Plantilla Item, Salary Grade, Promotion Track, Career Progression Level, Performance Rating, Competency Assessment, Position Competency Profile, Application, Documentary Requirement, Reclassification Request, Personnel Movement, Service Record, Audit Log.

## Required Approvals

Eligibility or document validation, evaluation review, HRMPSB action where applicable, approving or appointing authority action, records validation for service record updates.

## Outputs

Promotion assessment record, career progression tracker, reclassification request tracker, recommendation records, appointment or movement record, updated service record, audit report.

## Audit Trail

Log evidence submission, competency assessment linkage, performance rating linkage, evaluation updates, recommendation, approval, reclassification decision, personnel movement, and service record update.

## Permissions

Separate permissions for viewing promotion evidence, managing career progression, evaluating reclassification, approving actions, updating service records, exporting reports, and viewing audit logs.

## Policy Dependencies

DepEd higher teaching position, expanded career progression, amendment or clarification issuances, DepEd memoranda, CSC rules, Qualification Standards, PRIME-HRM, CBHRMS.

## Future Automation

Promotion readiness dashboard, competency gap summaries, career path planner, reclassification evidence checklist, service record draft generation, and succession readiness analytics.
