# Reporting Architecture

Reports must support operational tracking, compliance evidence, strategic HR management, and audit review.

## Report Families

- Vacancy and publication reports.
- Applicant, application, and documentary requirement status.
- Qualification standards checking reports.
- Comparative Assessment Result reports.
- CAR / CAR-RQA / RQA-style outputs where applicable.
- HRMPSB evaluation and ranking reports.
- Appointment recommendation and appointment tracking reports.
- Promotion, career progression, and reclassification reports.
- Personnel movement and service record update reports.
- Competency mapping, gap analysis, IDP, and L&D reports.
- PRIME-HRM readiness and CBHRMS maturity reports.
- Audit log and document history reports.

## Report Rules

- Reports must be office-scoped and permission-gated.
- Exports must be logged.
- Generated reports should include filters, generation time, requesting user, and office scope.
- Reports containing personal data must show only the fields needed for the purpose.
- Official output templates must be verified manually against current DepEd and CSC requirements before implementation.
