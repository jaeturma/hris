# Hiring, Selection, and Appointment Architecture

This architecture covers Vacancy and Publication through Appointment Tracking.

## Purpose

Support DepEd-first recruitment, selection, appointment, and placement workflows with policy-aware records, competency references, HRMPSB evidence, ranking outputs, appointment tracking, audit trail, and privacy controls.

## Actors

Applicant, HR staff, records officer, evaluator, HRMPSB member, HRMPSB chair, approving authority, appointing authority, auditor.

## Required Records

Vacancy, Publication, Applicant, Application, Documentary Requirement, Qualification Standard, Evaluation Criteria, Evaluator, Assessment, Interview, HRMPSB record, Ranking, Comparative Assessment Result, CAR / CAR-RQA / RQA-style output, Appointment Recommendation, Appointment, Audit Log.

## Required Approvals

Publication approval, evaluation validation, HRMPSB recommendation, ranking or output certification where applicable, appointment recommendation, and appointing authority action.

## Outputs

Publication notices, application lists, documentary checklists, QS results, initial evaluation results, comparative assessment results, ranking lists, CAR / CAR-RQA / RQA-style outputs, recommendation records, appointment tracker reports.

## Audit Trail

Log publication, application submission, document verification, QS checking, assessment changes, interview records, ranking generation, output export, recommendation, and appointment status changes.

## Permissions

Separate permissions for vacancy management, applicant viewing, document verification, evaluation, HRMPSB review, ranking generation, appointment recommendation, appointment tracking, export, and audit viewing.

## Policy Dependencies

DepEd recruitment, selection, and appointment issuances; relevant memoranda; CSC Qualification Standards; Data Privacy Act; government records management.

## Future Automation

Completeness checks, QS checklist assistance, scoring calculation after policy verification, report template generation, deadline reminders, duplicate applicant detection, and audit anomaly summaries.
