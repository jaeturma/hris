# Architecture Roadmap

## Phase 1: Core Foundation and Organization Structure

Establish office hierarchy, users, roles, permissions, audit trail, reference data, and governance standards.

## Phase 2: Position, Plantilla, and Competency Framework

Build plantilla, position titles, salary grades, job families, competency model, competency dictionary, proficiency levels, behavioral indicators, position profiles, and position-to-competency mapping.

## Phase 3: Employee Profile and 201 Files

Build employee profile, government employment details, 201 file metadata, service record foundation, and privacy controls.

## Phase 4: Recruitment, Selection, and Placement

Support qualification standards, competency-based applicant evaluation, HRMPSB evidence, merit selection plan alignment, and placement fit.

## Phase 5: Performance Management and Competency Assessment

Support SPMS alignment, performance rating linkage, competency assessment, behavioral indicators, and gap analysis.

## Phase 6: Learning and Development / IDP

Build Individual Development Plans, learning interventions, competency gap closure tracking, and L&D reporting.

## Phase 7: Promotion, Reclassification, and Succession Readiness

Support promotion readiness, reclassification evidence, succession planning readiness, competency evidence, and policy-aware workflows.

## Phase 8: Rewards and Recognition

Support PRAISE alignment, rewards evidence, recognition records, performance linkage, and demonstrated competency linkage.

## Phase 9: Leave, Attendance, and Payroll Preparation

Support leave management, attendance records, payroll preparation data, and audit-safe HR operations.

## Phase 10: Reports, Analytics, PRIME-HRM Readiness, and AI Assistance

Support compliance reports, workforce analytics, competency analytics, PRIME-HRM readiness dashboards, and AI-assisted decision support with human review.
