# Rewards and Recognition Domain

## Purpose

Own award programs, nominations, evaluations, recognition records, PRAISE alignment, award history, and evidence linkage.

## Business Scope

Award Program, Nomination, Evaluation, Recognition, PRAISE Alignment, Award History, Competency and Performance Evidence.

## Out of Scope

Performance rating ownership, competency assessment ownership, payroll benefits, and document storage ownership.

## Main Entities

Award Program, Nomination, Recognition Evaluation, Recognition, PRAISE Alignment, Award History, Evidence Reference.

## Aggregate or Ownership Candidates

Award program, nomination, recognition record.

## Key Business Rules

- Recognition must be evidence-based and aligned with PRAISE where applicable.
- Performance and competency evidence are referenced through snapshots.
- Award decisions require approval history and audit.

## Commands or Use Cases

Open award program, submit nomination, evaluate nomination, approve recognition, record award history.

## Domain Events

AwardProgramOpened, NominationSubmitted, RecognitionEvaluationCompleted, RecognitionApproved, AwardRecorded.

## Dependencies

Depends on Workforce, Performance, Competency, Personnel Records, Policy and Configuration, and Governance.

## Policy Dependencies

PRAISE, PRIME-HRM Rewards and Recognition, DepEd local recognition policies, Data Privacy Act.

## Security Sensitivity

High.

## Audit Requirements

Log nominations, evaluations, committee actions, approvals, award changes, and evidence access.

## Reports Produced

Award program report, nomination list, awardee list, recognition history, PRIME-HRM rewards evidence.

## Implementation Phase

Phase 8.
